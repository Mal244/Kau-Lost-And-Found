Have you ever lost something at the university? You didn’t know what to do and how you could get it back right? You could check the Faculty GroupChat but you lost it in another faculty that you aren’t in or a student that isn’t in your faculty group found it. That’s an issue! There’s no single place on university to report or search for them. You can lose your wallet in library and ask the front desk about it but a student who found it posts it on the group chat. We both never meet so items sit unclaimed and the owner (You) give up looking.

KAU Lost and Found is our website that gives every student in the university a one place to look for. A student can create an account, log in, and read how reporting works or browse our current list of recently found items with place and date each was found. Our site has four pages (Home, Login, Sign up and About us) Built with HTML,CSS,JS. It is published on github so you can freely look at the source code and anyone can open it.
