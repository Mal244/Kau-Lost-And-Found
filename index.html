The problem

If you leave your wallet in the library, you might check the front desk while the student who found it posts in a class chat. A campus-wide list gives you both one place to look.

How it works

  1. Create an account or log in.
  2. Describe the item and where you lost or found it.
  3. Look for a matching report and confirm a detail before arranging a handover.

Benefits

  • Reports reach students outside your own class.
  • Locations and dates help you narrow down possible matches.
  • Finders can describe an item without sharing private details from a student ID.
